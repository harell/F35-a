But · the game's streets there are fake

On top of what? Not the procedural grid

In both frames on the left the streets are the game's procedural grid: 105 × 76 m blocks in a random orientation, with nothing to do with Herne Bay's real streets. Real houses dropped onto it would sit across fake roads. The roads layer under these models has to be the real one: either extend the aerial photo square west to cover both areas (the photo already carries every street, driveway and pool, as it does from Westhaven's east end to the port), or extend the CBD's real-street region (LINZ centrelines painted by the terrain shader). The photo is the cheaper and better-looking choice; the toggle "Aerial on the ground" shows it.

Ground · the game's terrain, not the LiDAR

Each building keeps its own base height

Roofs are stored with their base (y0, the lowest LiDAR ground under the outline). When porting, each building moves by one offset, game terrain minus y0 at its centroid, so it sits on the same ground as the road layer and its roof stays level. The game terrain was not compared with the LiDAR for these two areas yet; on the Domain the high tier was within about 2 m.

What the models are made of

Measured, read off a photo, or guessed

MeasuredEvery building's outline (OSM, the LINZ import), base height and roof: flat, gable, hip, or hipped along any outline, with eave height and pitch fitted to the 1 m LiDAR surface by least squares. Roofs that step are split into terraces. Buildings OSM misses are traced from the LiDAR. Each tree's position, height and crown (LiDAR watershed). Boat decks, cabins and masts (LiDAR).
From photosRoof colours, tree colours, and at Westhaven each boat's outline, heading and colour and every pontoon and finger (the 2024 aerial, 0.3 m).
GuessedWall colours (a light palette: no 3D mesh covers these suburbs), eaves without overhang, crown shape and base, hull shape. The LiDAR and the photo were flown on different days, so a berth can hold a boat in one and not the other; masts are 15 cm wide and the 1 m LiDAR misses some, so some yachts read as launches.
Porting, after this review

What each layer becomes in the game

BuildingsSwap into the LINZ building list like the Scene apartments (applyHeroBuildings), with a new pitched-roof prism kind, so scenery, collision and collapse follow. Or ship the merged mesh per area as a small baked binary.
TreesA baked list (x, z, height, crown, colour) drawn with the game's instanced trees; lift the "no trees on the aerial" rule inside these footprints.
BoatsReplace the procedural yachts at Westhaven with the measured list, instanced like the trees. The pontoons replace the OSM walkways there.
GroundExtend the aerial square west over Herne Bay and Westhaven, and switch off procedural houses and trees inside both footprints.
