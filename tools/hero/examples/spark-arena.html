Spark Arena, rebuilt from LINZ data in code

The game today shows Spark Arena only as a photo on the ground. This page builds it in 3D from public LINZ LiDAR and aerial imagery, with photos used only to check materials. Drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom.

How it was made · about 40 minutes, mostly automatic

Five steps from public data to a game-ready model

STEP 1 · AUTOMATIC

LiDAR heights

LINZ 2024 Auckland LiDAR, 1 m surface minus ground, read straight from the public nz-elevation bucket. Roof tops out at 31.1 m above ground.

STEP 2 · AUTOMATIC

7.5 cm aerial photo

LINZ 2024 urban aerial, tiles BA32_1000_3605/3606. Shows the two lens-shaped roofs, the white walkway between them, the glazed foyer and the wing.

STEP 3 · AUTOMATIC

Fit the roof

A tilted plane fitted to each lens matches the LiDAR to 0.25 m (north) and 0.32 m RMS (south). That confirms the roofing supplier's description: "two elliptical tilted planes".

Each lens is 122 × 48 m. The north one falls from 27 m to 18 m; the south one from 28 m to 16 m.

STEP 4 · NEEDS A HUMAN

Check materials in photos

Contractor and street photos give the ribbed standing-seam roof with its dark overhanging edges, the silver ribbed walls, the curved blue foyer glass over a covered entrance, the white struts under the roof tips, and the "Spark ARENA" LED sign with its starburst (coral by day, purple at night). The ribs are drawn at 630 mm, the width of the roof sheet the supplier names. Two of the eight "Spark Arena" photos I scraped were of other venues, so a person has to check this step.

STEP 5 · CODE

Build it in three.js

About 250 lines that turn the measurements above into meshes. The game already builds the Sky Tower this way, in src/core/skyTower.ts. No 3D modelling tool and no hand-sculpted mesh.

What the model is made of

Measured, read off the photo, or guessed

MeasuredRoof planes, tilt and heights; building height; the flat walkway between the lenses (all from LiDAR).
From photoLens tips and outline, rim positions, foyer curve, wing and plant deck positions (from the aerial).
From street photosFoyer layers (entrances, canopy, glass band, silver drum), wall ribbing, dark roof edges, the struts under the roof tips, the sign and starburst and their day and night colours.
GuessedExact strut count and spacing, the sign's exact size and position, the south-west wing's glazing, and the east and south walls, which no photo shows.
