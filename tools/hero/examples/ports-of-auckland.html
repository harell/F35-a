How it was made · about 2 hours with the hero-building tools

From public data to a port the game can use

THE GAME TODAY

A frame from the running game

Dev build, high quality, looking south-west across Fergusson. The same meshes are exported into stage 1.

STEP 1 · AUTOMATIC

LiDAR over 2.4 km

tools/hero/site.py: 1 m DSM and DEM and the 2024 aerial. OSM gives the port's outline (relation 11815188) and 74 sheds. The wharf deck reads 3.2 m everywhere.

STEP 2 · AUTOMATIC

Cranes, masts and stacks

tools/hero/sites/ports_of_auckland.py finds each crane's quay edge, girder, apex, backreach, outreach and boom state. It takes masts from compact spikes. Each stack group gets its own grid orientation, 2.6 m tiers and greedy-merged blocks.

STEP 3 · NEEDS A HUMAN

Check against photos

Ten Commons photos give the crane colours, white legs and dark blue booms, and confirm the two crane classes. The aerial and the LiDAR were flown on different days, so the booms disagree; the LiDAR wins.

STEP 4 · CODE

Build it in three.js

One parametric ship-to-shore crane and one prism builder for decks and sheds. The stacks are boxes with the aerial on top. The game's own CBD mesh stands around the port, so only the port differs between stages.

What the hero model is made of

Measured, read off a photo, or guessed

MeasuredCrane positions, axes, girder and apex heights, backreach, outreach and boom state; stack footprints and tiers; mast positions and heights; shed roof heights; deck height (LiDAR). Port and shed outlines (OSM).
From photosCrane colours and the A-frame, machinery-house and boom layout; stack side colours come from the aerial.
GuessedCrane leg gauge (30.5 m) and leg spacing, the raised booms' length, shed wall colours, the mast lamp heads. Container doors and corrugations aren't modelled.
Putting it in the game

What's left after this review

Shape dataBake cranes, masts and stack blocks into a small binary like the LINZ buildings file. Stacks are about 10k boxes: merge them into one mesh, and use a cheaper LOD (the yard blocks as single prisms) far away.
ReplaceDrop the random stacks, the Bledisloe cranes and the 14 floodlights from buildRealPort. Keep its berth faces: the ships alongside are sim entities.
CollisionCranes are the tallest things on the waterfront after the CBD towers (123 m with a boom up): give them collision boxes.
