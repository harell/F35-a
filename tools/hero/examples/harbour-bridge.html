How it was made · about 2 hours with the hero-building tools

From public data to a model the game can use

THE GAME TODAY

A frame from the running game

Dev build, high quality. The deck is a dark slab and the truss is a small hump at the north end. The same mesh is exported into stage 1.

STEP 1 · AUTOMATIC

LiDAR profile

tools/hero/site.py: 1 m DSM and DEM over 1.3 × 1.3 km. Sampled across the deck every metre along the pier line. The deck is two straight ±5.0 % grades joined by a 248 m vertical curve, crest 46.4 m at s = −35 m.

STEP 2 · AUTOMATIC

The truss from above

Two chords sit ±7.5 m from the centreline and peak at 64.4 m. The top bracing repeats every 15.3 m (autocorrelation), so 16 panels across the 244 m main span. A 73 m spike is the pair of flagpoles.

STEP 3 · NEEDS A HUMAN

What's under the deck

Five Wikimedia Commons photos show what LiDAR can't. A deck truss runs the whole length, deepest over the main piers. The clip-on box girders curve down to brackets on the piers. The piers are rough concrete shafts with a steel cap. Spans come from Structurae: 80.8 · 103.7 · 114.4 · 124.1 · 177.3 · 244.0 · 176.9 m.

STEP 4 · CODE

Build it in three.js

About 300 lines turn these numbers into meshes: 7 spans, 68 truss panels a side, two lofted clip-on girders, 6 piers, lamps, gantries and flags. That's about 40k triangles in 6 materials, so 6 draw calls.

What the hero model is made of

Measured, read off a photo, or guessed

MeasuredDeck profile and width, top-chord profile and spacing, panel length, where the through truss starts and ends, flagpole height, sign-gantry positions (LiDAR). Pier positions and footprints (OSM, from the LINZ chart).
From docsSpan lengths and abutment positions (Structurae), 35.4 m width and the 13.4 m + 2 × 11 m split (Wikipedia).
From photosA deck truss along the whole length, deeper at the main piers; clip-on girders with curved haunches; pier shape and steel caps; lamp posts on the outer edges; pale grey paint.
GuessedTruss depth below the deck (8 m on the approaches, 16 m at the main piers), clip-on depths (3.4 m, 10 m at the main piers), the diagonal pattern, lamp spacing, the seabed depth at the piers.
Putting it in the game

What's left after this review

Shape dataMove the measured numbers into src/core/harbourBridge.ts. Fix bridge_n in AKL: the deck reaches Northcote Point 56 m further on, still 34 m up, so the motorway ribbon has to meet it there.
MeshesReplace buildHarbourBridge with merged meshes per material. Use a cheaper truss far away, because the 68 panels a side are most of the triangles.
CollisionDeck, through-truss envelope and piers as flyable obstacles, so flying under the main span means real 43 m clearance.
