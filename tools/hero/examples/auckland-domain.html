The ground is closer than it looks: on the high tier the game's terrain is within about 2 m of the LiDAR over most of the park. What the game lacks is everything standing on that ground. Half the Domain is canopy up to m tall, and today an aircraft flies straight through it.

How it was made · about 1 hour with the hero-building tools

From public data to a park the game can use

STEP 1 · AUTOMATIC

LiDAR over 1.5 km

tools/hero/site.py: 1 m DSM and DEM and the 2024 aerial. OSM gives the park boundary (way 6029919), 29 buildings inside it and 8 ponds.

STEP 2 · AUTOMATIC

One crown per tree

tools/hero/sites/auckland_domain.py: canopy where the surface stands 2.5 m over the ground. Tops are local maxima, with a wider window for taller trees. A watershed splits the canopy into crowns, each coloured from the aerial.

STEP 3 · AUTOMATIC

Buildings and ponds

Each OSM outline is raised to its LiDAR roof. The two glasshouses get a barrel vault: the cross-sections rise from about 6 m at the walls to 12 m at the ridge. Each pond is a water surface at its ground level.

STEP 4 · CODE

Build it in three.js

The trees are two instanced meshes, crowns and trunks. The ground is the 3 m DEM under the aerial, with the museum's footprint filled. Outside the park stand the game's own CBD meshes, so the park is all that differs.

What the hero model is made of

Measured, read off a photo, or guessed

MeasuredGround (1 m DEM); every tree's position, top height and crown spread; building roof heights; the glasshouses' eave and ridge; pond levels (LiDAR). Park, building and pond outlines (OSM).
From photosCrown colours and the ground, paths, fields and roofs: the 2024 aerial.
GuessedCrown shape (a rounded ellipsoid), crown base at 30 % of the tree's height, crowns widened 10 % to close the gaps left by the watershed, trunk size, the glasshouses' vault being a smooth arch. The museum's footprint is filled with one tone taken from the ground around it.
Putting it in the game

What's left after this review

TreesBake the crowns into a small binary like the port stacks (about 2,100 records), drawn with the game's broadleaf and conifer instances. Lift the "no trees under the aerial" rule inside the park only.
CollisionTrees over about 20 m are real obstacles for a low pass. Give the canopy a coarse height grid in the sim's terrain lookup.
BuildingsSwap the 29 park outlines into the LINZ building list (applyHeroBuildings), the glasshouses as a bespoke vault.
MuseumOut of scope here. The game's box stands about 80 m north of the real building and should move when the museum gets its own model.
