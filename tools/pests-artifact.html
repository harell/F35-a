Canopy Division · Brigadier

Brushtail possum

Trichosurus vulpecula · 1.4–6.4 kg

Enlisted
1837, shipped from Australia to start a fur trade
Strength
Tens of millions, in most of the country's forests
Orders
Strip the canopy by night

Field record

  • Browses rātā, kāmahi, tōtara and mistletoe until trees die back.
  • Takes eggs and chicks from nests, and the fruit and flowers native birds need.
  • Spreads bovine tuberculosis to cattle and deer.

On the model

Grey morph: silver-tipped back, cream throat and belly, dark muzzle bridge, big oval ears, and the bushy black tail with its bare grasping strip underneath.

Mast-Year Infantry · Sapper

Ship rat

Rattus rattus · 120–160 g

Enlisted
Mid-1800s, off European ships
Strength
Booms after beech and rimu mast years
Orders
Climb everything, raid every nest

Field record

  • The most widespread rat in New Zealand forest, and an expert climber.
  • Eats eggs, chicks, wētā, snails and lizards, plus the seeds forests need to regrow.
  • A plague of rats feeds a plague of stoats the following year.

On the model

Grey-brown agouti coat with ticked tips, pale belly, thin near-hairless ears, long whiskers, and a ringed, scaly tail longer than the body.

Special Operations · Commando

Stoat

Mustela erminea · 200–360 g

Enlisted
1880s, brought in to control rabbits
Range
Mainland-wide, from beach to alpine tops
Orders
Kiwi chicks. Then everything else.

Field record

  • The leading killer of kiwi chicks in the wild.
  • Hunts hole-nesting birds such as mohua, kākā and kākāriki.
  • Can swim more than a kilometre to reach a predator-free island.

On the model

Long, low body on short legs, chestnut back with a straight line to the cream belly, and the black tail tip that tells a stoat from a weasel. The in-game target in mission g03 is a low-poly cousin of this model.

Air Wing · Squadron Leader

German wasp

Vespula germanica · worker, about 14 mm

Enlisted
1940s; the common wasp followed in the 1970s
Strength
Among the densest wasp populations on Earth, in South Island beech forest
Orders
Hold the honeydew

Field record

  • Takes the beech honeydew that kākā, tūī and korimako depend on.
  • Eats huge numbers of native insects and spiders.
  • Controlled with Vespex protein bait in late summer.

Predator Free 2050 targets possums, rats and mustelids. Wasps are not on its list, so in the Codex this unit fights on as an unofficial ally.

On the model

Banded gaster with the German wasp's central black points and paired dots, three black dots on the yellow clypeus, faceted eyes, elbowed antennae, and veined wings. Shown enlarged: turn on Wing beat to see them move.

Models are procedural: signed-distance sculpts meshed in the browser, shell fur combed along the body, and generated textures for wing veins, eye facets and tail scales. No photos or scanned assets. Each model is true to scale in metres; the viewer frames each one to fill the stage.

Predator Free 2050 is the New Zealand goal, set in 2016, to eradicate possums, rats and mustelids (stoats, ferrets and weasels) by 2050.
