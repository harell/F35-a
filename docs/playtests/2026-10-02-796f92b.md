**Top time sinks and what was done about each**

1. **Browser time per mission** (1.2, 1.3, 2.2): a page load per mission was ~24 s plus 8–14 s per screenshot under load, so 24 missions took about 21 minutes. Blinking cues fell on their off phase in screenshots.
   *Built:* `browser-run.mjs --missions=a,b,c` (one page, `fly()` per mission, about 2.3× faster), `--shots=0`, `--text` (HUD `fillText` strings over 8 frames), and a two-frame wait before state is read (stale `renderer` counts, 1.2-c).
2. **Reaching a HUD or flow state** (1.3, 1.4, 2.2, 2.3): reviewers had to keep the player alive (health, subsystems and incoming missiles all reset), force a tank loss or an objective, pin the camera with a `rig.update` override, and wait about 60 s for the debrief after `simulate()`. Two charter states (tank lost, missile hint) were never reached.
   *Built:* `__f35.invulnerable()`, `destroy(id | 'group')`, `camera(pos, look)` and `skipOutro()`, all inside the TEST_HOOKS-only `debugApi()`. With them, 1.3-c was checked in game in under 30 s.
3. **Bot and sweep gaps** (1.1, 2.1): the sweep's `events` were always empty; there was no `--loadout`; IA ids weren't reproducible; the bot couldn't use the GBU-53 and always rearmed at Whenuapai. A reviewer had to write its own logged runner.
   *Built in r1/r2:* `--log`, `--loadout`, `--nojitter`, mixed `--missions` groups, IA ids seeded from the id, the bot on the GBU-53 and `rearmHome(def)`.

**Scheduling.** Round 1 ran 3 browsers and a 2-job sweep on 4 cores, which put the load average at 7–11 and made screenshots 2–3× slower. What worked:
- a frozen-snapshot worktree on its own port, so reviewers tested while developers fixed;
- developer subagents per area in their own worktrees, merged by cherry-pick;
- starting round 2's charters before round 1's last fixes landed.

All of this is now in `playtest/SKILL.md` (parallel limits, frozen snapshot, per-area developers, verification under load) and `charters.md` (repros that force the state).

**Also learned** (now in `play-f35`):
- `simulate()` freezes the HUD clock.
- `autopilot(true)` picks its own weapon.
- Don't `pkill -f` a pattern that's in your own command line. It killed the orchestrator's own shell once in this run.
- Exploit probes are about 50× faster headless.
- Pinning the jet trips overstress damage.
